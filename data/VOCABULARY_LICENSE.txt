The vocabulary-expansion.js dataset and grammar-ready-vocabulary.js
construction metadata are adapted from English Wiktionary contributors,
using Kaikki's Wiktextract extraction. The authors of each entry are
attributed through the linked Wiktionary page and its history.

Modifications: filtered to Punjabi Gurmukhi base entries with explicit
source romanization and English glosses; grouped meanings; assigned stable
IDs and provisional topic tags; omitted quotations, audio and conjugations;
manually reviewed direct-object metadata for a separately identified subset.

input checksum, snapshot dates, counts, exclusions and quality limitations.
